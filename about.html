About OLE Foundation

Learn more about our mission, our history, and the people who make our work possible.

Our story

Founded in 2015, OLE Foundation began with a single soup kitchen in Cape Town. Today, we operate in three cities, providing integrated support: food, clothing banks, and transitional shelter. We have grown from a small team of volunteers to a dedicated staff of 20, serving over 2,400 people annually.

Mission

To uplift vulnerable communities through essential resources and compassionate care. We believe in restoring dignity, one meal, one garment, and one safe night at a time.

Vision

A world where everyone has access to a warm meal, a decent outfit, and a safe place to sleep. We envision communities where no one is left behind.

Founded in 2015 in partnership with Gift of the Givers, OLE Foundation began with a single soup kitchen in Cape Town. Today, we operate in three cities, providing integrated support: food, clothing banks, and transitional shelter. We have grown from a small team of volunteers to a dedicated staff of 20, serving over 2,400 people annually.
